What I found

01

The homepage takes a long time to appear in the load test.

In a simulated slow-connection load test, your homepage's first content appeared at 11.3 seconds and its largest part, the main heading, at 21.2 seconds. The browser was also tied up for 620 milliseconds, and the page is about 3.2 MB. Three files (two jQuery scripts and a share-button stylesheet) hold the page back for an estimated 2.4 seconds, and the largest single file is a 0.7 MB animated image from arbrea-labs.com. One run.

02

The homepage title is 108 characters and names towns where you have no office.

It reads “Plastic Surgeon | Las Vegas NV Cosmetic Surgery | Breast Augmentation | Bullhead City, Laughlin, Pahrump, NV”. The pages list two offices, Las Vegas and Lake Havasu City, AZ, and Lake Havasu isn't in the title. In my search for your name, your homepage came back with this title.

03

Office hours aren't on any page, and one call link is written differently.

None of the 14 pages I saved lists office hours, and the business markup has none. The markup lists the Las Vegas office only, not Lake Havasu City. The “Call Us” button's link is written “tel: 702.870.7070”, with a space after the colon. I didn't test it. The other call links are plain digits.

04

Small fixes from my accessibility check.

It scored your homepage 82. It found 15 links and buttons in blue that are hard to read against the cream background (contrast 3.58), 8 links with no readable name, 3 images with no alt text, and 1 heading out of order. The page's setup also tells browsers to block zooming. I didn't test that on a phone.

05

Your /llms.txt address returns a web page.

It returns a full web page (about 100 KB) with a normal “200 OK” instead of a text file. It's an optional file that some AI tools read to understand a site, so this is a small gap.

What's working

A page for each procedure in your menu (facelift, tummy tuck, mommy makeover, breast augmentation and more), your Las Vegas and Lake Havasu City addresses and phone numbers on every page I saved, a consultation form with name, email and phone, an accessibility statement, and package prices on the Specials page. Your homepage has a description and a canonical address. Your page sitemap lists 88 pages and all 88 load, and robots.txt blocks nothing. When I searched your name, four of the five results were your own pages.

Unmeasured

Visitor numbers, rankings, how the site looks and loads on a phone (untested), whether the contact form works, what the RealSelf Reviews page shows once its scripts run, the 423 blog posts in your sitemap (I tested the 88 pages only), and speed on pages other than the homepage. I didn't submit any forms or look at private data.
